# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian adventure seekers (20–45) researching treks, camping, and tours on phone first, often after seeing Instagram or WhatsApp recommendations. They want to feel the trail before they pay — then book a seat with a clear UPI advance and human confirmation.

Secondary users: returning guests checking bookings, and friends/family browsing for a group trip.

## Product Purpose

Phoenix Adventures is a full-stack adventure booking platform for India. The public site inspires and converts: discover expeditions across the Sahyadris and Himalayas, pick a date and seats, pay advance via manual UPI, and get confirmed after admin verification. Success is emotional commitment on first scroll plus a clean path from “I want this trek” to a submitted payment.

## Brand Personality

**Cinematic · Grounded · Adventurous · Tribe**

- Cinematic: full-bleed landscape, quiet drama
- Grounded: local guides, real trails, honest pricing and safety
- Adventurous: bold CTAs, terracotta accents, motion that feels like movement on a trail
- Tribe: “Discover the great outdoors with our adventure tribe”

Voice: confident and local — not corporate travel SaaS, not luxury brochure fluff.

Established: **22 March 2023**. Contact and trust stats: see `CONTEXT.md`.

## Anti-references

- Generic travel booking SaaS (flat card grids, blue primary, form-first UX)
- Purple / indigo gradient startup landing pages
- Dark-mode-only cinematic kits (site is light cream + forest + terracotta)
- Glassmorphism marketing kits and “AI SaaS” hero templates
- Identical icon+heading feature card grids with no imagery
- Invented awards / magazine “Featured in” strips

## Design Principles

1. **Show the trail first** — Photography and place carry the brand; UI never outshines the landscape.
2. **Inspiration → booking in one scroll arc** — Every section earns a step toward Book / Pay via UPI.
3. **Trust is local and specific** — Guide credibility, ratings, and safety before generic badges.
4. **Motion = movement** — Framer Motion for intentional choreography (hero entrance, icon micro-motion, sticky book cues); never decorative fade-on-every-section.
5. **Thumb-first on mobile** — Sticky booking actions, 44px targets, single-column discovery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on body and CTAs
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables Ken Burns, parallax, and non-essential icon loops
- Focus-visible rings on all interactive controls
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px on mobile
- Hindi/Marathi language support remains available where wired
